Dashboard on a phone

Round 3, between rounds 1 and 2: hosts and moderators run the party from a phone. Round 1’s calm stacked rows, one status headline and calendar scheduling, with round 2’s device picker, health facts, labelled overflow menu and no horizontal overflow. Tickets #418, #419; feedback U1, U5.

Playing in the party Changed

The band stacks: the status headline, then the player unit straight on the band (art, title, progress, transport, rate, health facts), then the Up Next panel, then Upcoming Songs with each Request on its own flat row, vote capsule and estimated time beneath, the overflow menu top right.

  • Upcoming badge reads “Sent to Player” (GLOSSARY.md term, same as Requests), replacing the built “Sent to Spotify”.
  • Upcoming rows stack: title and artist take the full width; length, votes and the actions sit beneath (critique P0 “tiled” Dashboard, #419).
  • Schedule and Remove move into a labelled overflow menu, 44px targets, so a thumb cannot hit Remove by accident (#419, A-H7).
  • Health facts wrap as a quiet row of label and value pairs; the Status cell is gone because the headline says it; ages in words (U1).
  • The 4px horizontal overflow from .row.g-4 is gone: gutters are zero in the stacked layout (#419).
  • From round 1: the large headline with its dot (“Playing”), the unboxed player, flat borderless Upcoming rows with a rounded vote capsule, “About 21:44” start times and the yellow “Plays at 21:40” pill on a Scheduled Request.
  • From round 2: the player unit with transport and rate, the Up Next panel, the real VoteControl, the health facts (Player, Device, Last update, Spotify link, Polling), the labelled overflow menu with 44px targets and no horizontal overflow.
  • Shared round-3 pieces: state pill on Start Playback, moving bars in the Playing headline, shared attention count and all-clear empty state.
  • The Playing bars loop because they mean live and stop under reduced motion.

Schedule a Request New

On a phone the schedule picker is a bottom sheet instead of an anchored popover: the same month calendar, time field with quick choices, plain sentence and Schedule button, with full-width tap targets.

  • From round 1: calendar scheduling reached from the row overflow menu (“Schedule: Pick a day and time for it to play”).
  • The sheet dims the page, closes with the handle, Cancel or Escape, and keeps the Schedule button inside the thumb zone (#419).
  • Days, quick choices and buttons are 44px tall.
