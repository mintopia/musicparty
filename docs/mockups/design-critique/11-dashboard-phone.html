Music Party mockups

Dashboard on a phone

Round 2: hosts and moderators run the party from a phone. Stacked Upcoming rows, a labelled overflow menu for row actions, device choice and health visible without a fold, no horizontal overflow. Tickets #418, #419; feedback U1, U5.

Playing in the party Changed

The band stacks: player unit (art, title, progress, transport, rate, then the status grid as two columns), then Up Next, then Upcoming Songs with each Request on its own stacked row.

  • Upcoming badge reads “Sent to Player” (GLOSSARY.md term, same as Requests), replacing the built “Sent to Spotify”.
  • Upcoming rows stack: title and artist take the full width; length, votes and the actions sit beneath (critique P0 “tiled” Dashboard, #419).
  • Schedule and Remove move into a labelled overflow menu, 44px targets, so a thumb cannot hit Remove by accident (#419, A-H7).
  • Health grid is a two-column list including the new Status cell; ages in words (U1).
  • The 4px horizontal overflow from .row.g-4 is gone: gutters are zero in the stacked layout (#419).

Nothing playing Changed

  • Device picker keeps two columns; with more devices it wraps to rows.
