Design critique mockups

Dashboard: one playback status

Ticket #418. The four overlapping playback messages (top pill, Start Playback panel, Needs attention row, health grid) collapse into one status band at the top of the Dashboard. It answers “is music coming out, and if not, what do I do?”, and its actions are the next steps. Polling and device facts live in a collapsed Diagnostics disclosure.

Playing

The status band is the now-playing strip: album tint, headline, what is playing and where it comes from, plus the controls the moderator needs most.

  • One stage band replaces the pill, panel and health grid. The signal dot beside the headline is the only status colour.
  • Controls sit inside the status as its actions: Pause, Skip track, Open Web Player.
  • The Needs attention card now only holds Flagged Requests; playback problems can no longer appear there.
  • Ages are words: “3 seconds ago”, “45 days ago”. No raw minutes anywhere.

Not playing, with next steps

Same band, same place; headline and actions change. Amber signal.

  • “Open Web Player” is the primary light button (it works on any laptop). “Start on Spotify” wears Spotify green and says whose account it will run on.
  • The detail line gives the cause and the age in words, and reassures that guests can keep adding Requests.

Player not reporting (stale)

When updates have stopped we can no longer claim music is playing or stopped, so we say so and offer ways to get a Player back. Red signal.

  • Different headline, not an extra banner: the stale case replaces the status instead of stacking on it.
  • Three next steps in priority order: Open Web Player, Turn on Polling, Start on Spotify.

Diagnostics expanded

Collapsed by default everywhere; this frame shows the same Not playing state with it open.

  • Facts are label/value pairs with a small qualifier, in the app’s own vocabulary (Player, Polling, Context Playlist).
  • The summary row says what is inside so moderators know when to open it.

Phone

The status band stacks: headline, detail, then full-width 44px actions. The queue is in 02-phone-dashboard.html.

  • Art tile is dropped under 576px to give the headline and detail the full width.
  • Needs attention comes before the queue on phone so Flagged Requests are not buried.
