Member list Changed

One search field above a full-width table replaces the Search card and its Nickname and Role filters. It matches name and role as plain words, understands typed filters, and shows the same filters as role chips. The table gains Requests, Votes and Flagged counts, and joins are in words.

  • Round 3: page head 40/800 and card titles 650 for a clear type step; empty states use the record-sleeve icon in the colours of the cover now playing.
  • Combined search (decision 7, #426): field accepts `role:vip` or plain words; the chip row mirrors it and shows counts. No filter sidebar. Clear a filter by clicking its chip.
  • Role chips are Owner, Moderator, VIP, User, Banned (decision 6 adds Moderator).
  • Columns Requests, Votes and Flagged are new data per member; "Flagged" is orange only when above zero. Joined is in words (decision 11).
  • Row actions live in a labelled overflow ("Actions for Ravi"): Change role, View activity, Ban. Reason: moderation actions are rare and risky, so they are not loose buttons on every row.
  • Full width because the table is dense (decision 12). Page title and summary use `.mp-page-head`; there is one `h1`.
  • Dark frame is the app dark mode; the role badges use the Tabler light tints.

Member detail and role Changed

The detail page now shows what a host needs to judge a member: stats, their Requests with status, their votes, and any Flagged Requests with the reason. Change role is a page with one line per role.

  • Detail: Requests, Votes and Flags are `h2` sections under the member name `h1`. Each Flagged Request names why it was flagged (rule or AI review criterion) and links to that review. Reason: U4, U5, A-H7.
  • Role picker explains each role in one line. Moderator is marked New and states what it can and cannot do (decision 6): remove, approve or reject Requests, schedule them, read AI reviews; no Settings, no role changes, no starting playback on the owner's Spotify. The backend role is a later ticket.
  • Today's hints are kept for Owner, VIP, User and Banned, tightened. Reason: U5.
  • Ban confirm uses the built `ConfirmDialog` pattern and names the member and the consequence; the button repeats the action ("Ban Ravi").
  • Open question: should Ban also remove the member's waiting Requests from the Queue? Mocked as no.

Moderator view New

A Moderator (Dev) sees Members but cannot change anything: no row actions, no Change role or Ban, and Settings is missing from the sidebar. A short note says who can.

  • Decision 6: Members is read-only for Moderators. The sidebar lists Dashboard, Queue, Search, Requests, Moderation, Members; no Settings.
  • The note replaces the missing buttons so the page does not look broken. A detail page for a Moderator is the same as the owner's without the two action buttons.
  • Open question to the lead: may Moderators see the Flagged count per member? Mocked as yes, since they review flags.
