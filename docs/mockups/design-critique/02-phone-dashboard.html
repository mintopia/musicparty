Phone Dashboard: Upcoming Songs

Ticket #419. At 390px every Upcoming row gives the title and artist the full width on their own lines, with time and votes underneath. Schedule and Remove move into an overflow menu whose items carry visible labels. The desktop layout is unchanged.

Phone

Each row is art, text, overflow button on the first line; time and votes on the second. Nothing is a fixed-width column except the 48px art and 44px buttons.

  • Titles and artists wrap at word boundaries and are never truncated; “Everybody (Backstreet’s Back) (Extended Version)” fits on three lines.
  • Time and duration sit left, votes right, directly under the text, so the whole row is one thumb-reachable block.
  • The overflow button is 44px. Its menu rows are 44px tall with an icon, a visible label and a one-line reason; Remove is red and last.
  • Votes use the shared vote control at its 44px touch size. The rows have no horizontal overflow at 390px.
  • A compact Playing strip stays above the page title; the full status band from #418 is hidden at this height of scroll, not duplicated.

Desktop (unchanged)

At 992px and up the row keeps its single-line grid: position, art, text, time, votes, then Schedule and Remove as icon buttons with tooltips and aria-labels.

  • Schedule and Remove stay as icon buttons on desktop; the divider keeps the destructive Remove away from votes.
  • Implementation: one list, two presentations by breakpoint (below 768px the overflow menu replaces the two icon buttons).
