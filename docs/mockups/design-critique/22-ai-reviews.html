Music Party mockups

Moderation: AI reviews

The AI reviews tab and review detail, with combined search instead of the filter sidebar (#426; U4, U5, U7). Outcomes and criteria follow ADR 0014.

AI reviews list Changed

The third Moderation tab. The filter sidebar is gone: one search field matches every text column and takes typed filters, and the Outcome and Criterion chips set the same filters. Same width as Flagged Requests and Rules.

  • Sidebar of three fields removed; combined search with Outcome and Criterion chips instead, counts on each chip. #426, U7, U4.
  • Columns stay: Outcome badge (icon + word, never colour alone), Criterion, Track, Requested by, Reason (two lines), plus When in words. Time moved to its own column; before it sat under the name.
  • Criterion reads "None" instead of a dash; the criterion only labels a flag or reject, as in ADR 0014.
  • The "Flagged requests aren't held" notice is the one on the Flagged tab; it appears on this tab too when approval is off.

Filtered and empty Changed

Typing outcome:rejected presses the Rejected chip. With no reviews yet the page says what fills it.

  • The result count in the field makes the filter visible: "3 of 39 reviews".
  • An empty page explains what fills it. When a search matches nothing it uses "No matching reviews" with Clear filters, as on Requests. When the AI Request Review mod is off, this page is replaced by the "AI reviews are off for this party" page (41-errors).

Review detail Changed

The detail page starts with the verdict and then shows how it was reached: the score against its three bands, the model's confidence against the party's threshold, the chosen criterion, and what happened to the Request. Technical details stay, collapsed.

  • The detail is a sub-page of Moderation: its h1 is the track, with a back link to All AI reviews instead of the tabs. A scale from 0 to 4 with the Reject (below 0.75), Flag (below 2.5) and Allow bands and a marker for this score. ADR 0014 bands. U4.
  • Confidence is a separate bar with the party's threshold as a tick, and a sentence that says which side it fell on. Low confidence allows the Request whatever the score (fail-open), which the second frame spells out.
  • Criterion keeps the probability bars and says it never changes the decision.
  • "What happened to the Request" links onward: a flagged Request gets Approve and Reject in the page head, the primary action while it is still waiting; once decided the head offers Open Request. Moderators can do both. U5.
  • Model, latency and tokens move to a Review details card; the request payload and raw response stay collapsed. The old Model answers table becomes the Criterion bars.
  • Scale maximum of 4 is assumed from five ordered levels; check against the model before building.

AI reviews on a phone Changed

The list becomes cards with the outcome badge and reason; the detail stacks the verdict first, then what happened, then how it scored.

  • Chips wrap; the Outcome and Criterion groups keep their labels. Tap targets are 44px.
  • On the detail the Approve and Reject buttons come right after the verdict because a moderator on a phone is usually here to decide.
